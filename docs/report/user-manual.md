# Maize Disease Checker — User Manual

A simple guide for farmers using the Maize Disease Checker app on a smartphone.

---

## What is Maize Disease Checker?

This app helps you check if your maize leaves are healthy or sick. Take a
photo of a leaf, and the app will tell you in seconds if it is healthy or
diseased. The app works on any smartphone with a camera and internet
connection. It also works without internet.

---

## Installing the app on your phone

### On Android

1. Open Chrome on your phone.
2. Go to the app website.
3. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
4. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
5. Tap "Add" or "Install".
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

### On iPhone

1. Open Safari on your phone.
2. Go to the app website.
3. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
5. Tap "Add".
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

---

## How to use the app

- **Step 1:** Open the app from your home screen.
- **Step 2:** Tap "Scan Leaf".
- **Step 3:** Your camera will open — point it at a maize leaf.
- **Step 4:** Take a photo of one leaf at a time.
- **Step 5:** Wait a few seconds while the app checks the leaf.
- **Step 6:** Read the result on the screen.

---

## Understanding your results

### GREEN screen says HEALTHY

- Your leaf looks healthy.
- No disease found on this leaf.
- This is a good sign.

### RED screen says DISEASED

- Your leaf may be sick.
- Check other leaves on the same plant.
- Check leaves on nearby plants too.
- If many leaves are diseased, speak to an agricultural officer.

### The percentage number (example: 85%)

- This shows how sure the app is about its answer.
- 90% or higher = the app is very sure.
- 60–89% = the app thinks it is likely correct, but check again.
- Below 60% = try taking the photo again in better light.

---

## Tips for better photos

- Take photos in good daylight — not in the shade or at night.
- Hold the phone still when taking the photo.
- Make sure the whole leaf fills most of the photo.
- Clean the camera lens if photos look blurry.
- Take one leaf photo at a time.

---

## Using the app without internet

- The app works even without mobile data or WiFi.
- You will see a yellow bar at the top saying "Offline mode".
- The app uses a smaller model stored on your phone — results may be
  slightly less accurate.
- Your scans are saved automatically and you can view them later.

---

## Viewing past scans

- Tap "History" at the top of the screen.
- You can see your last 20 scans.
- Each scan shows: the date, whether the leaf was healthy or diseased, and
  how sure the app was.

---

## Troubleshooting

### My camera does not open

- Make sure you have allowed the app to use your camera.
- On Android: Settings → Apps → Maize Disease Checker → Permissions →
  Camera → Allow.
- On iPhone: Settings → Maize Disease Checker → Camera → On.

### The app is slow or not responding

- Close the app and open it again.
- If you have no internet, the app will use offline mode automatically.

### The result does not look right

- Try taking the photo again in better light.
- Make sure the leaf fills the photo.
- If you are still unsure, take a sample of the leaf to an agricultural
  officer.

---

## Your privacy

- We do not store your photos.
- Your photos are checked and then deleted from our system.
- We remove your phone's location information from photos before sending
  them.
- Your scan history is saved only on your phone.
